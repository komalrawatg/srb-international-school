Director's Message

This is the first time your child will be stepping out of home and the shadow of your doting care. Preschool age is the most important and impressionable phase in a child's life.

From various researches and studies it has been proved that more than half of the child brain develops during the first four years of age. The growth of the brain is so rapid that a child is able to take in almost everything, provided it is given to him in the form in which he can understand.

SRB INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL provides a completely safe and secure environment-an environment that promotes independence, meaningful play, social interaction and more importantly a love for learning-that sets the course for his lifetime of learning.

We hope this brochure gives you a glimpse of how we work together, children and staff to create and maintain a sense of community of security and of collaboration of how we put spirituality at the centre of what we do everyday and how we encourage thinking, success and celebration.

ABOUT SRB INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL

Nurturing Minds, Shaping Global Leaders

SRB International School, Surir (Mathura) is an English Medium Co-educational. We are committed to provide quality education in a safe, disciplined and nurturing environment with modern facilities, experienced faculty and holistic development of every child.

We work passionately to develop critical logic analytics framework setups, spatial mindset parameters and continuous character assessment indicators inside every young talent.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution of academic excellence with strong values, developing independent future thinkers who contribute positively to changing worldly spheres.

Our Mission

To nurture young minds to become responsible global citizens through quality framework environments, technical tool logic integration and standard community matrices.

Our Values

Discipline, Integrity, Respect, Excellence and Compassion guide our institutional journey and daily organizational pathways with supreme clarity.
