ABOUT SRB INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL

Nurturing Minds, Shaping Global Leaders

SRB International School, Surir (Mathura) is an English Medium Co-educational Residential & Day School affiliated to CBSE. We are committed to provide quality education in a safe, disciplined and nurturing environment with modern facilities, experienced faculty and holistic development of every child.

We work passionately to develop critical logic analytics framework setups, spatial mindset parameters and continuous character assessment indicators inside every young talent.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution of academic excellence with strong values, developing independent future thinkers who contribute positively to changing worldly spheres.

Our Mission

To nurture young minds to become responsible global citizens through quality framework environments, technical tool logic integration and standard community matrices.

Our Values

Discipline, Integrity, Respect, Excellence and Compassion guide our institutional journey and daily organizational pathways with supreme clarity.
